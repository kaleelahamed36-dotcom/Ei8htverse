Care guide

General guidance for our handmade crafts, charms and jewellery. Always follow any care notes listed on a specific product.

  • Put jewellery on after perfume, lotion, hairspray and make-up.
  • Remove it before swimming, bathing, workouts and sleep unless the product page says otherwise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing.
  • Store pieces separately in a dry pouch or box to avoid scratches and tangles.
  • Keep away from harsh chemicals, strong heat and damp places.
  • Handle charms, clasps and chains gently; avoid pulling or bending them roughly.
  • Pipe cleaner crafts are delicate: keep them away from water, heat and direct sun, handle gently and dust with a soft, dry brush.
